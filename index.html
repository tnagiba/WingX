Magellan Pace vs Market

How this is calculated: both lines are monthly departure counts divided by that series' own January to September 2025 average, so 100 means a typical 2025 month and 120 means 20% above it. Market = Part 135 charter jet departures worldwide, from WingX. Magellan = flown US jet departures from the Salesforce data warehouse, one per flight leg, cancellations and test accounts removed. Indexing each series to itself removes the scale difference and shows whether Magellan is growing faster or slower than the market it operates in.
